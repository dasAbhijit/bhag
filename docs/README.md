# Bhag

Bhag is a simple way to split bills with friends and family. Add an expense, say who paid and who shares it, and Bhag keeps track of who owes whom. Everything is in rupees (₹).

**Open the app:** https://dasabhijit.github.io/bhag/

## What you can do

- Split a bill **equally, by exact amounts, by percentage, or by shares**.
- Record bills **paid by more than one person**.
- Keep **unlimited groups** (a trip, a flat, a regular dinner) and **one-on-one** tabs with a friend.
- **Simplify debts** so a group settles with the fewest payments.
- Set up **repeating expenses** such as rent or subscriptions.
- Attach **receipt photos** to an expense.
- See your **combined balance** with each friend across every group.
- Use it on **iPhone, Android and the web**, with changes showing up live on everyone's device.
- **Export** a group to a spreadsheet (CSV).
- See an **activity log** of every change and leave **comments** on any expense.
