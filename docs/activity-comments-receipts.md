# Activity, comments and receipts

## Activity

The **Activity** tab lists every change in the group: expenses added, edited or deleted, payments, comments, and people joining. Edits show what changed, for example "Amount: ₹2,000.00 → ₹2,400.00".

## Comments

Open any expense and write in **Comments**. Everyone in the group sees them straight away. You can delete your own comments.

## Receipts

On an expense, tap **Add photo or PDF**. Photos are shrunk on your phone before they are uploaded. PDFs up to 5 MB also work. Receipts are stored privately and only people in the group can open them. Bhag does not read the text on receipts.
