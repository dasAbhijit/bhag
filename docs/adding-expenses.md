# Adding expenses

Open a group and tap **+ Add expense**. Enter a description, the amount in ₹, and the date. Then say who paid and how to split it.

## Who paid

By default it is you. Pick someone else from the list, or tick **Several people paid** and enter what each person paid. The amounts must add up to the total. A line under the list shows what is still to assign.

## How to split

| Method | Use it when | Example (₹4,000 bill) |
|---|---|---|
| **Equal** | Everyone shares the same. Untick people who were not part of it. | 4 people: ₹1,000 each |
| **Exact** | You know each person's amount. They must add up to the total. | Asha ₹2,000, Dev ₹1,200, you ₹800 |
| **%** | Shares are given as percentages. They must add up to 100. | 50%, 30%, 20% gives ₹2,000, ₹1,200, ₹800 |
| **Shares** | Proportions like 2:1:1. | 2, 1, 1 gives ₹2,000, ₹1,000, ₹1,000 |

Amounts that do not divide evenly are rounded to the paisa, and the extra paise are handed out fairly so the total always matches exactly.

## Repeating expenses

Choose **Repeat** on a new expense to add it every week, month or year. Each one is added on its date and split the same way as the first. Month-end dates are handled: a bill on the 31st lands on the 28th, 29th or 30th in shorter months.

Manage schedules in **People, then Repeating expenses**: pause, resume or delete. To change one, delete it and set up a new one.

## Editing and deleting

Open an expense to **Edit** or **Delete** it. A deleted expense stops counting towards balances. Anyone in the group can **Restore** it from the **Activity** tab.
