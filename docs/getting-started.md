# Getting started

## Sign in

Open https://dasabhijit.github.io/bhag/ and choose one of:

- **Continue with Google**, if your administrator has turned it on.
- **Email link**: enter your email and open the link we send. No password needed.
- **Password**: create an account with an email and a password.

## Your first group

1. On the home screen tap **+ Group** and give it a name, for example "Goa trip".
2. Open the group and tap **+ Add expense**.
3. Open the **People** tab and share the **invite link** so others can join.

## Joining a group

Open the invite link you were sent and sign in. If the person who made the group already added you by name, pick your name from the list. Your share of earlier expenses is then already there.
