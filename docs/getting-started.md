# Getting started

## Sign in

Open https://dasabhijit.github.io/bhag/ and choose one of:

- **Continue with Google**, if your administrator has turned it on.
- **Email link**: enter your email and open the link we send. No password needed.
- **Password**: create an account with an email and a password.

## Your first group

1. On the home screen tap **+ Group** and give it a name, for example "Goa trip".
2. Open the group and tap **+ Add expense**.
3. Open the **People** tab and share the **invite link** so others can join.

## Joining a group

Open the invite link you were sent and sign in. If the person who made the group already added you by name, pick your name from the list. Your share of earlier expenses is then already there.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in screen choose **Password**, type your email, and tap **Forgot password?**
2. Open the email we send **on the same device and browser** and tap the link.
3. Choose a new password and save. You are signed in.

The email can take a minute. If nothing arrives, check spam and try again after a short wait.

## Change your password

Open **Account**, then **Password**.

- **Change password:** type a new password (at least 8 characters) twice and save.
- **Email me a reset link:** use this if the app asks you to confirm it is you. It sends the same link as above.

If you usually sign in with an email link or Google, setting a password also lets you sign in with your email and that password.
