# Groups and friends

## Groups

Create as many groups as you like. Each group has its own expenses, balances, activity and members.

In the **People** tab you can:

- **Add a friend by name** before they have an account. They show as "not joined yet". When they open the invite link they can claim their name and keep everything already recorded for them.
- **Copy or share the invite link.** Anyone with the link can join after signing in. Choose **Make a new link** to stop an old link from working.
- **Rename or remove** friends who have not joined, or leave the group yourself. Someone who appears in an expense or payment cannot be removed until those are deleted.
- Turn **Simplify debts** on or off for the group.

Only the person who created a group can delete it. Deleting removes everything in it for everyone.

## One-on-one

Tap **+ Friend** on the home screen to keep a running tab between the two of you. It works like a group of two.

## Friends tab

The **Friends** tab shows your net balance with each person, added up across every group you share. Tap a name to see the amount in each group.

People are matched across groups by their account. A friend who has not signed up yet only appears in the group where you added them.
