# Install and export

## Put Bhag on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the app in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the app in Chrome, open the menu, then **Install app**.

It opens full screen like any other app. You need an internet connection to see and change balances.

## Export to a spreadsheet

In a group, open **People** and tap **Export to spreadsheet (CSV)**. The file has one row per expense or payment and one column per person, showing what each person paid minus what they owe for that row. The last row is the total balance. Open it in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.
