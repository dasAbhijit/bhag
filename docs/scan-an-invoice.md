# Scan an invoice

Take a photo of a bill and Bhag fills in the shop, date, total and every item. You then say who had what, and Bhag works out each person's share, including tax and tip.

## One-time setup: your Gemini key

Scanning uses Google Gemini with your own API key, so there is no charge from Bhag.

1. Get a key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey).
2. In Bhag open **Account**, then **Invoice scanning**.
3. Paste the key and tap **Save**, then **Test key** to check it works.

The key is saved only in your browser, on that device. It is never stored on Bhag's server, and you add it again on each device you use. **Remove key** deletes it.

## Scanning a bill

1. Open a group and tap **+ Add expense**.
2. Tap **Scan invoice** and take a photo, or choose one from your gallery.
3. Wait a few seconds. Bhag fills in the description, date, category and all the items, and switches the split to **Items**.
4. **Check it.** Fix any name or price it misread. Add or remove items.
5. For each item, tap the people who had it. Everyone starts selected. Tap **None** and then the names to pick only some.
6. **Tax, service charge and tip** is one amount for everything that is not an item. It is shared in proportion to what each person's items came to. Use a minus sign for a discount.
7. Check **Each person owes**, then **Save expense**.

The photo is saved with the expense as its receipt. The items stay saved too, so you can open the expense later and change who had what.

## Tips for a good scan

- Lay the bill flat in good light and fill the frame. Long bills read better in one straight photo than a tilted one.
- If the items and the tax do not add up to the printed total, Bhag says so. Look for a missed or misread line.
- Handwritten bills and crumpled thermal paper are the hardest. You can always fix things by hand.

## Privacy and limits

- The photo is sent to Google when you scan. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products, so avoid scanning anything private. Check Google's current terms.
- Free keys have usage limits. If you hit one, wait a little and try again.
- Amounts are read as rupees. If the bill says another currency, Bhag warns you.
- Scanning needs an internet connection.
