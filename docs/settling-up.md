# Settling up

## Balances

The **Balances** tab shows each person's net position. Green means the group owes them. Amber means they owe the group.

## Simplify debts

With **Simplify debts** on, Bhag combines IOUs so the group settles with the fewest payments. If A owes B ₹100 and B owes C ₹100, A pays C ₹100 directly and B is not involved.

With it off, you see the direct amounts people owe each other.

## Recording a payment

Under **Settle up**, tap **Settle** next to a suggested payment, check the amount, and save. Use **Record a payment** for anything else. Payments show in the expense list and reduce what is owed. If you record one by mistake, delete it from the list.

Bhag does not move money. Pay by UPI, cash or however you like, then record it here.
